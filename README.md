# IdeaVerse – AI Creative Thinking Universe

### Where Human Creativity Meets Artificial Intelligence

**IdeaVerse** is an interactive, AI-powered brainstorming application designed to help people explore ideas, discover unexpected connections, and develop creative thinking through colorful visual mind maps.

Inspired by the playful and interactive experience of *The Sims*, IdeaVerse transforms brainstorming into a dynamic, exploratory experience.

## The Concept

Rather than simply generating answers, AI acts as a creative thinking partner.

Users can build visual mind maps while AI contributes new ideas, questions, connections, and alternative perspectives directly within the map.

**The core principle: AI expands human creativity without replacing human decision-making.**

## Key Features

- **Interactive Mind Maps:** Create, connect, edit, and move colorful idea bubbles.
- **AI-Powered Brainstorming:** Generate ideas, questions, connections, and perspectives.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Review, keep, edit, or dismiss AI-generated suggestions.
- **Notes & Insights:** Generate AI insights based on written notes.
- **Collaboration:** Share boards and collaborate with other users.
- **Multilingual Experience:** Support for English, Hebrew, and Arabic.
- **Undo & Redo:** Explore ideas with greater flexibility and control.
